# RealTime CTMS Competitive Analysis & Reverse Engineering

**Suite:** Clinical Trials & Patient Recruitment CRM  
**Secondary Competitor:** RealTime-CTMS (RealTime Software Solutions)  
**Analysis Date:** 2026-11-03  
**Target Profile:** Clinical Research Sites, Dedicated Trial Centers, and Site Management Organizations (SMOs)  

---

## 1. Product & Business Overview

### 1.1 Company & Positioning
- **Market Standing:** Industry standard CTMS specifically built for clinical research *sites* (rather than pharma sponsors). Over 4,000+ trial sites use RealTime globally.
- **Core Value Proposition:** End-to-end site operations management: recruitment, subject scheduling, regulatory compliance (eDOCS), investigator payments (Pay), and electronic source data (eSOURCE).
- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** Independent clinical trial sites, dedicated research networks, hospital clinical trial units.

### 1.2 Pricing & Packaging Architecture
- **Pricing Model:** Subscription per active site and active protocol, typically custom-quoted.
- **Estimated Range:** $500–$1,500/month per physical research site depending on active study volume and module add-ons (eSOURCE, eDOCS, Patient Pay).

---

## 2. Information Architecture & Workflows

### 2.1 Core Modules & Hierarchy
1. **Recruitment & Subject Database:** Central patient registry with therapeutic tags, past trial participation history, and status tracking.
2. **Visit Tracking & Subject Calendars:** Protocol visit schedules (e.g. Day 0, Day 14, Day 30), procedure checklists, and automated SMS appointment reminders.
3. **Financials & Site Budgeting:** Protocol payment milestones, investigator compensation, stipends, and sponsor invoicing.
4. **Regulatory (eDOCS):** 21 CFR Part 11 compliant binder management, CVs, medical licenses, IRB approval logs.

---

## 3. Customer Reviews, Pain Points & Operational Limitations

1. **Legacy User Interface:**
   - Users across Capterra and industry forums note the interface feels like early-2000s software with dense tabular grids and excessive sub-menus.
2. **Manual Protocol Schedule Setup:**
   - Coordinators must manually type in every single protocol visit procedure (e.g., blood draw, ECG, vital signs) from a 150-page protocol PDF.
3. **Weak Advanced Recruitment Intelligence:**
   - While patient demographic search exists, there is no AI-driven matching or automated protocol inclusion/exclusion extraction.
