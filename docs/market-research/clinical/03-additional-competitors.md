# Additional Clinical Trial Competitors & Landscape Analysis

**Suite:** Clinical Trials & Patient Recruitment CRM  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Competitive Landscape Matrix

| Product | Target Segment | Pricing Architecture | Strengths | Clinical Research Blind Spots |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Veeva Vault CTMS** | Global Top-50 Pharma & Mega-CROs | Enterprise licensing ($100K–$1M+/yr) | Gold standard for enterprise clinical governance, CDISC compliance, and regulatory submissions. | Massive overkill, unaffordable, and impractically complex for independent sites and early-stage biotech. |
| **Medidata (Dassault Systèmes)** | Mid-to-Large Trial Sponsors | Per-study enterprise contracts | Industry leader in Electronic Data Capture (EDC), Rave RTSM, and decentralised trials. | Focuses on clinical data capture rather than site-level patient recruitment CRM and coordinator workflows. |
| **CRIO (Clinical Research IO)** | Dedicated Clinical Trial Sites | Per-subject or per-study monthly subscription | Modern eSource, CTMS, and paperless site coordination. | Limited top-of-funnel marketing CRM and multi-channel campaign tracking. |
| **StudyTeam (OneStudyTeam / Deep 6)** | Sponsors & Investigative Sites | Enterprise sponsor-paid model | Excellent patient pipeline visibility between sponsors and participating trial sites. | Primarily an enterprise sponsor portal; lacks self-serve CRM pricing for independent research centers. |

---

## 2. Key Synthesis

- **The Big Divide:** Clinical trial tech is divided between ultra-expensive enterprise platforms (Veeva, Medidata) built for global pharma compliance and site-level tools (RealTime, CRIO) with legacy interfaces and weak top-of-funnel marketing CRM automation.
- **The AI Opportunity:** No mid-market platform automatically digests trial protocol PDFs to generate inclusion/exclusion prescreening rules, or warns site coordinators of patient drop-out risk based on visit adherence history.
