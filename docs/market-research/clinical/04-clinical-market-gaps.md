# Clinical Trials & CTMS CRM: Market Gaps & Opportunity Analysis

**Suite:** Clinical Trials & Patient Recruitment CRM  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Validated Unmet Needs & Operational Frictions

1. **Protocol-to-Prescreening Extraction Friction:**
   - *Current Reality:* Trial coordinators spend days reading 150-page protocol PDFs to manually draft pre-screening survey questions for prospective patient leads.
   - *Market Gap:* Automated AI parsing of protocol PDFs to extract inclusion/exclusion criteria into instant pre-screening forms.
2. **Patient Drop-out & Screening Failure Rates:**
   - *Current Reality:* Up to 70% of potential trial candidates fail screening visits, and 30% of enrolled patients drop out before trial completion, costing sponsors millions in delayed drug timelines.
   - *Market Gap:* AI-driven pipeline tracking that highlights eligibility red flags before scheduling costly on-site screening visits.
3. **Regulatory Consent Compliance Guardrails:**
   - *Current Reality:* Human error in protocol randomization (e.g. randomizing a patient before the IRB-approved Informed Consent Form is signed) results in major FDA audit findings and protocol deviations.
   - *Market Gap:* Code-enforced electronic consent validation that prevents accidental subject randomization without a verified ICF on file.
