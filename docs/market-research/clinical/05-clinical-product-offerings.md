# Clinical Suite: Differentiated Product Offerings

**Suite:** Clinical Trials & Patient Recruitment CRM  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Differentiated Capabilities for AI_CRM Clinical Suite

### Offering 1: AI Protocol Assistant & Automated Inclusion/Exclusion Extractor
- **Target Persona:** Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC) / Principal Investigator (PI).
- **Workflow:** User uploads an IRB-approved protocol PDF. AI extracts core patient eligibility criteria (age, biomarker thresholds, prior therapies, washout periods) and generates a patient-facing prescreening survey.
- **Differentiating Value:** Reduces trial launch prescreening setup time from weeks to minutes.

### Offering 2: Patient Recruitment Funnel & 21 CFR Part 50 Consent Guardrail
- **Target Persona:** Site Coordinator / Clinical Project Manager.
- **Workflow:** Real-time patient pipeline from social ad/referral lead to randomized participant (`Identified` ➔ `Prescreened` ➔ `Consented` ➔ `Randomized`). The engine strictly blocks progression to randomization without an auditable verified electronic consent form.
- **Differentiating Value:** Built directly into the CRM, preventing protocol deviations and reducing audit risk.

### Offering 3: Site Accrual & Retention Intelligence
- **Target Persona:** Trial Sponsor Operations / Site Network Director.
- **Workflow:** Live monitoring of site enrollment velocity against target study timelines, highlighting sites falling behind accrual quotas and predicting drop-out risk.
