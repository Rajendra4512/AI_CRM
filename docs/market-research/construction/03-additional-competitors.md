# Additional Construction Competitors & Landscape Analysis

**Suite:** Commercial Subcontractor & Trade CRM  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Competitive Landscape Matrix

| Product | Target Segment | Pricing Architecture | Strengths | Trade Subcontractor Blind Spots |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Autodesk Construction Cloud (PlanGrid / Build)** | Mid-to-Enterprise GCs & Design Teams | Per-user or unlimited sheets pricing ($550–$2,000/user/yr) | Industry-leading 2D/3D BIM sheet viewing, versioning, and mobile plan markups. | Design/architect centric; weak sales CRM, proposal pipeline, and trade estimating. |
| **Buildertrend** | Residential Homebuilders & Remodelers | Tiered monthly ($399–$1,099/mo) | Superior client portal, selections, scheduling, and homeowner communication. | Unsuited for commercial trades (no multi-prime contracts, no AIA G702 billing, no union payroll). |
| **Fieldwire (Hilti)** | Field Crews, Specialty Contractors & GCs | Free tier, then $39–$79/user/month | Lean, task-oriented mobile plan viewing, punch lists, and inspections. | Pure field tool; no preconstruction CRM, estimating pipeline, or accounting invoicing. |
| **Knowify** | Commercial Subcontractors (Trades) | $186–$400+/month flat | Dedicated to specialty trades; AIA billing, job costing, and deep QuickBooks integration. | Outdated UI; lacks modern AI document parsing and multi-vertical intelligence. |

---

## 2. Key Synthesis

- **The Missing Bridge:** Existing platforms force trade subcontractors to either buy massive GC platforms (Procore/Autodesk) that ignore trade estimation and cost controls, or residential apps (Buildertrend) that cannot handle commercial retainage and multi-tier submittals.
- **The AI White Space:** None of these competitors offer automated spec chunking (e.g. searching a 600-page specification PDF for Division 08 glazing requirements) or automated change-order margin erosion alerts.
