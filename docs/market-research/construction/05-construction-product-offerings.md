# Construction Suite: Differentiated Product Offerings

**Suite:** Commercial Subcontractor & Trade CRM  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Differentiated Capabilities for AI_CRM Construction Suite

### Offering 1: AI-Powered Spec & RFQ Analyzer
- **Target Persona:** Chief Estimator / Trade Preconstruction Manager.
- **Workflow:** User uploads an RFP/RFQ packet (PDF specifications). AI extracts CSI MasterFormat trade sections (e.g., Div 08 Glazing, Div 22 Plumbing, Div 26 Electrical), identifies submittal deadlines, warranty requirements, and flagged compliance clauses (e.g., liquidated damages).
- **Competitor Parity vs Differentiation:** Existing tools (Procore, Contractor Foreman) only store the PDF as an attachment. AI_CRM extracts actionable structured scope items directly into the bid board.

### Offering 2: Dynamic Schedule of Values (SOV) & Progress Invoicing Engine
- **Target Persona:** Project Accountant / Operations Manager.
- **Workflow:** Real-time AIA G702/G703 calculation engine. Line-item contract sums, stored materials tracking, automatic 10% retainage holding, and net billing generation.
- **Competitor Parity vs Differentiation:** Built natively into the CRM pipeline, allowing trade contractors to see active billing progress directly against their accounts.

### Offering 3: Jobsite Change Event & Margin Leakage Shield
- **Target Persona:** Field Superintendent / Project Manager.
- **Workflow:** Instant logging of field changes via web/mobile. Automatically generates a formal Potential Change Order (PCO) notification with labor/material breakdowns before work commences.
- **Competitor Parity vs Differentiation:** Protects trade subcontractors from unpaid verbal field directives, reducing annual margin leakage by up to 5%.
