# Maritime Suite: Differentiated Product Offerings

**Suite:** Maritime Logistics & Freight Brokerage CRM  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Differentiated Capabilities for AI_CRM Maritime Suite

### Offering 1: Dynamic Multi-Factor Freight Quote Engine
- **Target Persona:** Freight Sales Executive / Ocean Cargo Broker.
- **Workflow:** Instant quote generation across global trade lanes (ASIA-USEC, ASIA-USWC, EUR-ASIA, TRANSATLANTIC) incorporating base ocean rates, Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF), Terminal Handling Charges (THC), and Peak Season Surcharge (PSS).
- **Differentiating Value:** Reduces spot quote generation time from 45 minutes to 30 seconds, dramatically improving quote win rates.

### Offering 2: AI Carrier Rate Sheet Ingestion & Comparison
- **Target Persona:** Pricing Manager / Forwarder Operations Director.
- **Workflow:** Automatically ingests carrier PDF/Excel rate updates, normalizes trade lane pricing, and flags the lowest-cost routing options.
- **Differentiating Value:** Eliminates hours of manual data entry and prevents margin leakage on ancillary surcharges.

### Offering 3: Proactive Container Milestone & Demurrage Shield
- **Target Persona:** Customer Service Representative / Logistics Dispatcher.
- **Workflow:** End-to-end milestone timeline tracking (`GATE_IN` ➔ `VESSEL_LOADED` ➔ `IN_TRANSIT` ➔ `DISCHARGED` ➔ `CUSTOMS_CLEARED` ➔ `DELIVERED`) with automated alerts for port dwell times.
