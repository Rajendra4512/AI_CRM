## 2. Key Industry Vulnerabilities We Target
- **High Total Cost of Ownership:** Enterprise tools cost upwards of $20K–$100K/year with mandatory annual contracts.
- **Generic Horizontal CRMs Fail:** Salesforce and HubSpot cannot calculate construction retainage, clinical protocol GCP consent compliance, or maritime bunker adjustment factors (BAF) without hundreds of thousands of dollars in custom consultancy.
