## 2. Capabilities We Deliberately Will NOT Build (Exclusion List)

To preserve focus and avoid feature-bloat traps:

1. **Construction: Native 3D BIM Authoring**:
   - Leave 3D modeling to Revit/Navisworks. We integrate sheet PDFs, not 3D rendering engines.
2. **Clinical: Autonomous Medical Eligibility Decisions**:
   - Regulatory frameworks strictly mandate human investigator decision-making. AI provides screening red flags, never automated diagnosis or autonomous enrollment.
3. **Maritime: Global Customs Port Clearance Brokerage**:
   - Customs filing (ACE/ABI) requires country-specific customs broker licenses. We manage forwarder sales, quotes, and shipment milestones; we connect to third-party customs EDI rather than writing customs filing software from scratch.
