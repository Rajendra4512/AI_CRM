# Commercial Packaging & Pricing Strategy

**Report Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Unified Multi-Vertical Packaging Structure

All three industry suites share a standardized 3-tier commercial packaging framework:

| Tier | Price (Billed Annually) | Target Persona | Document AI Allowance | Support & SLA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter** | **$99 – $199 / month** | Small independent operators (1–5 users) | 25 documents / month | Standard community & email support |
| **Professional** | **$249 – $499 / month** | Growing midsize operations (6–20 users) | 150 documents / month | Priority support, 4-hour response SLA |
| **Enterprise** | **$699 – $999+ / month** | Multi-branch / high-volume operations | 500+ documents / month | Dedicated account manager, 99.9% uptime SLA, custom integrations |

## 2. Monetization Levers & Add-Ons
- **AI Document Ingestion Boost Packs:** $50 per additional 500 document pages analyzed.
- **Dedicated Single-Tenant VPC Deployment:** $1,500/month flat for enterprise customers requiring dedicated database instances.
- **Two-Way Accounting / ERP Sync Connector:** $99/month add-on (QuickBooks, Sage, Xero).
