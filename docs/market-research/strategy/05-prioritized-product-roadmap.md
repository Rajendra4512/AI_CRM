# Prioritized Product Roadmap (P0 - P3)

**Report Date:** 2026-11-03  

---

## 1. Roadmap Hierarchy & Criteria

- **P0 (Launch Essential):** Core operational table-stakes and primary AI differentiator.
- **P1 (High-Value Differentiation):** Capabilities that drive expansion revenue and word-of-mouth adoption.
- **P2 (Advanced Competitive Parity):** Deeper ecosystem and integration tools.
- **P3 (Future Expansion):** Enterprise multi-branch governance and predictive modeling.

---

## 2. Multi-Vertical Release Phases

### Phase 1: Commercial MVP Launch (Current Foundation + P0 Features)
- **Shared Platform:**
  - [x] Multi-tenant database persistence & Auth (Complete).
  - [x] Shared Accounts, Contacts, Leads & Deals Kanban (Complete).
  - [x] Document parsing (PDF/DOCX) & tenant-isolated vector RAG (Complete).
- **Construction (Commercial Subcontractor):**
  - [x] Schedule of Values (SOV) calculation engine with 10% retainage (Complete).
  - [ ] AI Spec & RFQ Analyzer: Auto-tagging CSI MasterFormat divisions from uploaded bid specs.
- **Clinical (CTMS & Recruitment):**
  - [x] Patient Recruitment Funnel & 21 CFR Part 50 Consent Guardrail (Complete).
  - [ ] Automated Inclusion/Exclusion screener generation from protocol PDFs.
- **Maritime (Freight Brokerage):**
  - [x] Multi-factor Spot Quote Engine (Base + BAF + THC + PSS) (Complete).
  - [x] Milestone shipment timeline tracker (Complete).

### Phase 2: Differentiation & Automation (P1 Features)
- **Construction:** Change Event mobile capture & automated PCO generation to protect against margin erosion.
- **Clinical:** Patient SMS appointment reminders and coordinator follow-up queue.
- **Maritime:** Automated ocean carrier PDF rate sheet parser and lane price normalization.

### Phase 3: Ecosystem & Scale (P2 Features)
- **Accounting Connectors:** Two-way sync with QuickBooks Online and Xero.
- **Tracking APIs:** Live ocean vessel AIS tracking and INTTRA container status webhooks.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Margin leakage and win-loss prediction models across pipelines.
