8. SECURITY RISKS & MITIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Risk: Insecure direct object reference (IDOR) across tenants.
  Mitigation: Server-side derivation of tenant_id from validated JWT; mandatory .filter(tenant_id).
- Risk: Cross-industry privilege escalation.
  Mitigation: Backend require_industry_entitlement dependency rejects unauthorized calls with HTTP 403.
- Risk: Browser tampering of industry parameter during registration.
  Mitigation: Whitelist validation of industry string ('construction', 'clinical', 'maritime', 'all').
